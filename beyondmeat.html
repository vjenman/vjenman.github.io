Beyond Meat Class Project

For my marketing management class, we were required to consider some potential strategic shifts for a given company. My group and I determined that most people who want meat alternatives want something close to meat in taste and feel, just without the negative health and environmental effects. This is how Beyonder Meat was born. While there were many complexities, I thought that Beyonder Meat would be the most interesting to explore, and had the potential to capture the most market share. I researched lab-grown meat, created the action plan, decision framework and recommendation matrix that helped us come to our conclusion. I designed the slides using a combination of elements and the signature Beyond Meat colors. We presented our project to the class, and answered questions about our recommendations from our peers and professor. The experience tested my research, design, presentation, and teamwork skills, and I had the opportunity to learn more about the lab-grown and alternative meat industries.
